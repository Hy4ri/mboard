Word frequency lists
Arabic contains 25,000 selected entries from FrequencyWords' 2018 50k list. English starts with its 25,000 selected entries, refreshes frequencies from the OpenSubtitles2018 training slices below, and adds 288 whole contractions (25,288 entries total). Non-word tokens are filtered; files are tab-separated.

Next-word predictions
next_en.tsv and next_ar.tsv contain observed totals and follower counts for 30,000 one-word contexts; context_en.tsv and context_ar.tsv contain 60,000 two-word contexts each. Rows retain up to eight followers. A <s> row stores sentence-start counts. The desktop replay datasets are excluded verbatim from training.
English training uses compressed byte ranges [0,125000000) and [150000000,325000000) of OpenSubtitles2018; Arabic uses [0,125000000) and [145000000,400000000). The intervening ranges supply held-out benchmark text. These tables and the refreshed English frequencies are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
jordanian.tsv and jordanian_next.tsv combine the original hand-curated vocabulary/continuations, cleaned Shami corpus Jordanian text, and independently generated synthetic Jordanian chat messages. Shami observations are weighted three times relative to synthetic observations; jordanian_next.tsv continuation counts use only the training split of Shami (about 79% of lines, grouped in 50-line blocks) while the rest is held out for benchmarking, and jordanian.tsv vocabulary still comes from the full corpus; the synthetic text is not a measured human corpus. Original curated rows receive explicit synthetic rank-based counts. The dialect setting enables these extra predictions and completions.
Shami: Kathrein Abu Kwaik, Motaz Saad, Stergios Chatzikyriakidis, and Simon Dobnik (2018), "Shami: A Corpus of Levantine Arabic Dialects", LREC 2018. Source: https://github.com/GU-CLASP/shami-corpus (Data/jordinian.txt). Its repository is licensed under Apache License 2.0; the bundled adaptations strip mentions, URLs, emoji and Arabic marks and extract word frequencies and n-gram counts. License text: assets/licenses/shami-Apache-2.0.txt.
Rebuild scripts, source attribution and sampling limitations: bench/lm/SOURCES.md. Benchmark-only Tatoeba and spelling-error datasets are not packaged or used to generate the keyboard assets.
Source: OPUS, https://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles/corpus/version/OpenSubtitles (P. Lison and J. Tiedemann, 2016).
