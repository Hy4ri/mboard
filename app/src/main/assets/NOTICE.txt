Word frequency lists
English and Arabic assets contain 25,000 selected entries each from FrequencyWords' 2018 50k lists, with non-word tokens filtered and space-separated fields converted to tabs.
Author: Hermit Dave and FrequencyWords contributors.
Source: https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
Upstream data: OpenSubtitles2018 via OPUS.

These word-list adaptations are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. This does not change the license of unrelated application code.
Note: upstream's MIT license covers its generator code, not its word-list content.

Next-word predictions
next_en.tsv and next_ar.tsv list up to three likely following words per dictionary word, counted from the first 120 MB (compressed) of the OpenSubtitles2018 monolingual corpora.
Source: OPUS, https://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles/corpus/version/OpenSubtitles (P. Lison and J. Tiedemann, 2016).
Distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0 together with the word lists above.
